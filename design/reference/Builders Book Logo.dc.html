The Open Spread — production mark
One mark, no alternates. Filled cover-forms with block cursor center. Single color, works reversed. All measurements below are the spec.
THE MARK
Two cover-forms, spines 3.5 u, terminals 2 u deep × 3 u thick. Block cursor 3.5 × 11 u, inset 2.5 u top and bottom. Grid: 24 u; glyph box 16 × 16 u.

C · cap midline
A — Icon glyph height = 1.15 × cap height of the wordmark (glyph box only, excluding the 24 u frame’s built-in margin). Open forms need the overshoot to match the caps optically.
B — Gap = 3 cursor widths, measured from the right cover’s terminal edge to the T stem. At this scale: 24px against a 36px glyph.
C — Vertical alignment: the icon’s optical center sits on the cap-height midline (baseline + ½ cap height). Never baseline-align the icon.
Apostrophe: true typographic apostrophe U+2019, never a straight tick. Kerned −1.5% into the r and +1% before the s so “Builder’s” reads as one word; at this depth and weight it cannot be mistaken for an acute accent or backtick. Wordmark is Source Serif 4 semibold, title case — closed.

CIRCULAR AVATAR CROP — GLYPH BOX = 62% OF DIAMETER
Glyph bounding box set at exactly 62% of avatar diameter (60px in 96px, 30px in 48px — measured on the visible glyph, not the artboard). Extreme points are the cover corners: at 62%, corner reach is 0.88 × radius — clears the circular crop with margin, no terminal clipping.
